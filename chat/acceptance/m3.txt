# M3: /who and /nick

## /who lists who is connected, in alphabetical order
alice < * Welcome guest1!
bob < * Welcome guest2!
alice < * guest2 joined
alice > /who
alice < * online: guest1, guest2

## /who forgets the clients that left
alice < * Welcome guest1!
bob < * Welcome guest2!
alice < * guest2 joined
bob hangs up
alice < * guest2 left
alice > /who
alice < * online: guest1

## /nick changes your name, and everybody is told, you included
alice < * Welcome guest1!
bob < * Welcome guest2!
alice < * guest2 joined
alice > /nick alice
alice < * guest1 is now known as alice
bob < * guest1 is now known as alice
alice > hi
bob < alice: hi
bob > /who
bob < * online: alice, guest2

## A nickname is 1 to 16 letters, digits, - or _, and must be free
alice < * Welcome guest1!
bob < * Welcome guest2!
alice < * guest2 joined
alice > /nick alice
alice < * guest1 is now known as alice
bob < * guest1 is now known as alice
bob > /nick alice
bob < ! nickname taken: alice
bob > /nick b@d
bob < ! invalid nickname: b@d
bob > /nick two words
bob < ! invalid nickname: two words
bob > /nick waytoolongnickname
bob < ! invalid nickname: waytoolongnickname
bob > still guest2
alice < guest2: still guest2

## The name is free again when its owner leaves
alice < * Welcome guest1!
bob < * Welcome guest2!
alice < * guest2 joined
alice > /nick alice
alice < * guest1 is now known as alice
bob < * guest1 is now known as alice
alice > /quit
alice < * bye
bob < * alice left
bob > /nick alice
bob < * guest2 is now known as alice
